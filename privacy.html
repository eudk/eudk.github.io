Privacy Notice

This notice explains the limited data processing used by this portfolio.

Who I Am

My name is Evgeniy Gordienko. This is my personal portfolio website.

Cookies

The portfolio itself does not set cookies and does not use advertising or cookie-based tracking.

Analytics (GoatCounter)

The front page uses GoatCounter for basic traffic statistics such as page views and referrers. GoatCounter does not place cookies or tracking identifiers in your browser. Requests may include technical information such as the page URL, referrer, IP address, browser, system, language, and screen width. GoatCounter processes this information to produce aggregated statistics and detect repeat visits without storing IP addresses or full user-agent strings.

See the GoatCounter privacy policy.

Browser Storage

The site uses your browser's localStorage to remember your Danish or English language preference. The projects page also temporarily caches public GitHub repository information to reduce repeated API requests. This data stays in your browser and can be removed by clearing the site's browser storage.

Project Information (GitHub)

The projects page requests public repository information from the GitHub API. When this happens, GitHub receives standard connection information such as your IP address and browser details. No GitHub login is required. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Contact Form (Formspree)

If you submit the contact form, your email address and message are sent to Formspree, which processes and forwards the submission to me. Formspree may also process standard request information such as your IP address, browser type, access time, and referrer for operating and securing its service. See the Formspree Privacy Policy.

Your Rights

I do not use your personal data for marketing and I do not sell it to third parties. You can request deletion of your contact form message by contacting me.
